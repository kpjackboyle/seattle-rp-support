Support Center

How can we help?

Submit a support request to the Seattle Roleplay Support Team. Provide as much information as possible so our staff can get you the right assistance.

Submit a request form

Your request will be handled within a 5 hour time window via direct messaging.

Before submitting

Be specific Include names, approximate times, and what happened.
Evidence helps For reports, keep screenshots or video ready if staff request them.
One request is enough Please don't submit multiple requests for the same issue.
Need immediate help? If your server has an active support channel, staff may be able to assist there.
